WHERE WE WORK
Our mission is to transform prosperity and resilience for all coffee farmers, traders, and processors across Uganda.

We partner with government agencies, district local governments, and community‑based organizations to support coffee farmers, traders, and processors in accessing markets, finance, and sustainable production practices across diverse contexts and geographies. Our roots are in Uganda, where we have strengthened the coffee value chain in partnership with 22 district local governments and the national Ministry of Agriculture, Animal Industry and Fisheries. More recently, our impact has attracted interest from other coffee‑producing countries, including Rwanda and Ethiopia, paving the way for future scale‑up across Africa.
