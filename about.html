Mission & Vision

We Empower the entire coffee value chain from grower to global market by spearheading regenerative practices, forging pathways to high-value markets, and mobilizing catalytic capital, all while championing policies that transform rural livelihoods into resilient prosperity.

To be a leading association empowering Buddu coffee producers and traders for sustainable growth and global competitiveness.

Our Team

Our Uganda-based team brings together a powerful blend of deep agronomic
field operations expertise, strategic public-private partnerships,
human-centered product design for post-harvest handling and processing,
rigorous research and data science, and cutting-edge digital and financial tools—all to build, deploy,
and scale solutions that drive prosperity and resilience for Uganda's coffee communities.

We partner with the Ministry of Agriculture, Animal Industry and Fisheries and district governments in 22 of Uganda’s busiest coffee‑growing districts, who co‑own our interventions, and work with us to design what and how we measure impact for effective decision‑making. These partnerships have helped us reach over 50,000 farmers with our solutions across 500+ cooperatives and processing hubs, and will continue to be the cornerstone of how we approach the path to national scale in Uganda.

Our Partners

Partnerships are the backbone of our work. We believe sustainable impact is achieved through strong partnerships.

Government Partners

We work with county and national governments to co‑design solutions.

  • Ministry of Agriculture, Animal Industry and Fisheries (MAAIF)
  • Uganda Coffee Development Authority (UCDA)
  • National Agricultural Advisory Services (NAADS)
  • District Local Governments – 22 coffee‑growing districts
  • Regional Coffee Cooperative Unions
  • Farmer Cooperative Societies
  • Primary Processing Hubs

Implementing & Research Partners

BUDDU Coffee collaborates with a diverse network of research institutions, NGOs, and agricultural extension providers to ensure every coffee farmer has a path to sustainable prosperity.

  • National Coffee Research Institute (NaCORI)
  • Makerere University – College of Agricultural Sciences
  • International Coffee Organization (ICO)
  • World Coffee Research
  • TechnoServe Uganda
  • Solidaridad East Africa
  • GIZ – Agricultural Finance & Coffee Value Chain
