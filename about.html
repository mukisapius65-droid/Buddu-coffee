Mission & Vision

We Empower the entire coffee value chain from grower to global market by spearheading regenerative practices, forging pathways to high-value markets, and mobilizing catalytic capital, all while championing policies that transform rural livelihoods into resilient prosperity.

To be a leading association empowering Buddu coffee producers and traders for sustainable growth and global competitiveness.

Our Team
We are committed to developing strong local capacity to deliver
long‑term impact for rural coffee growers, traders
and traders at scale.

Our Uganda-based team brings together a powerful blend of deep agronomic
field operations expertise, strategic public-private partnerships,
human-centered product design for post-harvest handling and processing,
rigorous research and data science, and cutting-edge digital and financial tools—all to build, deploy,
and scale solutions that drive prosperity and resilience for Uganda's coffee communities.
