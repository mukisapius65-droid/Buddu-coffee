Our Partnerships

Since 2020, BUDDU Coffee has partnered with district local governments, farmer cooperatives, extension agents, and coffee communities to improve productivity, market access, and quality of life for smallholder farmers, where the majority of Uganda’s coffee is grown and processed.

We partner with the Ministry of Agriculture, Animal Industry and Fisheries and district governments in 22 of Uganda’s busiest coffee‑growing districts, who co‑own our interventions, and work with us to design what and how we measure impact for effective decision‑making. These partnerships have helped us reach over 50,000 farmers with our solutions across 500+ cooperatives and processing hubs, and will continue to be the cornerstone of how we approach the path to national scale in Uganda.

Influencing Decision-making through Research

Research is the backbone of how we design solutions, and our findings are guiding how district governments, cooperatives, and extension services improve productivity, quality, and market access across Uganda’s coffee value chain.

We rely on a mixture of participatory research approaches to understand the contextual challenges facing coffee farmers, traders, and processors: Human‑Centered Design, in‑depth interviews, and implementation research.

WHERE WE WORK

We have Ugandan roots and global ambitions

GROWSince 2020, BUDDU has worked with district local governments and the Ministry of Agriculture, Animal Industry and Fisheries to improve productivity, quality, and market access for coffee farmers in Uganda’s coffee‑growing regions. Guided by a mission to reach every coffee farmer, trader, and processor, our focus is on scaling further within Uganda while adapting our model and capitalizing on our learnings to serve coffee communities in new geographies. We currently partner with district governments in Rwenzori and Elgon regions to test and adapt our solutions to support national coffee priorities, with ambitious plans to grow our footprint across East Africa in the coming years.
