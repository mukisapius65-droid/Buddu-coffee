Our Model

Our package of solutions is designed to support and empower coffee farmers, traders, and processors across their
journey—from seedling to sustainable market access.

We developed our package of solutions to address a twin challenge impacting outcomes for coffee farmers, traders, and processors within Uganda’s agricultural systems: delays in accessing markets and adopting sustainable practices, and gaps in the quality of post‑harvest handling and value addition. Our model is built around a simple Theory of Change: that coffee growers adopt climate‑smart practices and reach markets at the right time with high‑quality produce – which in turn drives better incomes, stronger cooperatives, and more resilient, sustainable coffee value chains. We co‑design these solutions with farmers, local cooperatives, and our government partners so that they are responsive, context‑appropriate, and comprehensively tackle the barriers that have long kept smallholder communities from thriving.

Farmers access markets at the right time and place, and receive targeted support if quality or yield risks are identified.

GROW empowers farmers with real‑time market intelligence via SMS and mobile alerts to make informed choices about when to sell, how to improve quality, and what sustainable practices to adopt. Through two‑way communication, farmers can ask questions, report issues, and receive targeted support or referral to extension services and buyers when a risk is detected during the growing or post‑harvest phase.

Join our GROW network →

Training and quality assurance for cooperatives and extension agents to improve post‑harvest handling and sustainable farming.

FIELDS provides structured training programs for coffee cooperatives and extension agents, focusing on post‑harvest handling, pest management, certification standards, and climate‑smart agriculture. Our platform tracks skill gaps and certifies trainers, ensuring consistent quality and sustainability across the coffee value chain.

Explore FIELDS training →

Data‑driven insights for government and industry to improve market infrastructure, financing, and policy.

PULSE aggregates anonymised data from farmer interactions, market transactions, and extension services to provide real‑time dashboards for government partners and industry stakeholders. These insights help optimise resource allocation, design better policies, and target investments where they are needed most – creating a more resilient and transparent coffee ecosystem.

WHERE WE WORK

We have Ugandan roots and global ambitions

GROWSince 2020, BUDDU has worked with district local governments and the Ministry of Agriculture, Animal Industry and Fisheries to improve productivity, quality, and market access for coffee farmers in Uganda’s coffee‑growing regions. Guided by a mission to reach every coffee farmer, trader, and processor, our focus is on scaling further within Uganda while adapting our model and capitalizing on our learnings to serve coffee communities in new geographies. We currently partner with district governments in Rwenzori and Elgon regions to test and adapt our solutions to support national coffee priorities, with ambitious plans to grow our footprint across East Africa in the coming years.
